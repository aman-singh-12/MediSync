Blood Pressure FAQ
Q: What is a normal blood pressure reading?
A: A normal blood pressure reading is typically around 120/80 mmHg.

Q: How can I prepare for a blood pressure test?
A: Avoid caffeine, exercise, and smoking for at least 30 minutes before the test. Sit quietly for 5 minutes before the reading is taken.

Q: What does high blood pressure mean?
A: High blood pressure (hypertension) means the force of blood against your artery walls is consistently too high. Consult your doctor for personalized advice.
