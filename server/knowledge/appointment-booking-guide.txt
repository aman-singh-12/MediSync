# MediSync Appointment Booking & Management Guide

## Booking an Appointment (Patients)
1. Navigate to the "Find Doctors" section from your sidebar.
2. Search for doctors by name, specialty, or clinic name.
3. Click on a doctor's profile to view their details.
4. Select an available time slot on their calendar.
5. Confirm the booking. You may need to provide symptom details and process a payment depending on the clinic policies.

## Canceling an Appointment (Patients)
1. Navigate to the "Appointments" (or Appointment History) section.
2. Find the upcoming appointment you wish to cancel.
3. Click the "Cancel" option next to the appointment. Note that cancellations might be subject to the clinic's cancellation policy.

## Managing Appointments (Doctors)
1. Go to the "Appointments" section in the Doctor portal.
2. View your upcoming schedule.
3. You can mark appointments as completed, or if necessary, reschedule them by communicating with the patient.
4. You can also view patient medical history directly from the appointment view before the consultation starts.
