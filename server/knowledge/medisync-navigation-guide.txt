# MediSync Application Navigation Guide

## Overview
MediSync is a healthcare management application with distinct portals for Patients, Doctors, and Administrators.

## Patient Navigation
When logged in as a patient, you can access the following sections from the sidebar or dashboard:
1. Dashboard: A quick overview of your health metrics and upcoming appointments.
2. Find Doctors: Use this page to search for doctors, view their profiles, and check their availability.
3. Appointments (or Appointment History): View past and upcoming appointments, and book new ones.
4. Medical Records: Access your prescriptions, test results, and health history.
5. Favorites: See your saved doctors.
6. Payments: View transaction history and pay for appointments.
7. Settings: Update your profile information, password, and preferences.

## Doctor Navigation
When logged in as a doctor, you can access the following sections:
1. Dashboard: Overview of today's schedule and patient statistics.
2. Appointments: Manage your upcoming patient appointments.
3. My Patients: View records and history of patients you have treated.
4. Availability: Set your working hours, break times, and unavailable days.
5. Patient Reviews: See feedback from your patients.
6. Financials: Track earnings and payouts.
7. My Profile: Update your professional details and consultation fees.

## Administrator Navigation
When logged in as an admin, you can access:
1. Admin Dashboard: System-wide analytics and overview.
2. Manage Users: View and manage all patient and staff accounts.
3. Verify Doctors: Review doctor applications, credentials, and approve them for practice.
4. Transactions: Monitor all system payments.
5. System Settings: Configure platform-wide rules.

## Using the Interface
* Sidebar Toggle: On mobile or smaller screens, use the menu icon in the top left to open the sidebar.
* Notifications: Click the bell icon in the top right to view clinical alerts or system messages.
* Support: Click the question mark icon in the top right to access help and support options.
* Logout: The sign-out option is located at the bottom of the sidebar.
