Diabetes FAQ
Q: What are common symptoms of diabetes?
A: Common symptoms include increased thirst, frequent urination, extreme hunger, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, irritability, and blurred vision.

Q: What is a fasting blood sugar test?
A: A blood sample is taken after an overnight fast. A fasting blood sugar level less than 100 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) is normal.

Q: How often should I check my blood sugar?
A: The frequency of blood sugar testing depends on the type of diabetes and the treatment plan. Your doctor will provide specific guidelines.
