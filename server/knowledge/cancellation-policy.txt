MediSync Cancellation Policy
1. Notice Period: We require at least 24 hours notice for appointment cancellations.
2. Late Cancellations: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be subject to a cancellation fee of $50.
3. How to Cancel: You can cancel your appointment via the MediSync app, website, or by calling the clinic directly.
4. Emergencies: We understand that emergencies happen. If you have a medical emergency, please let us know as soon as possible and the cancellation fee may be waived.
