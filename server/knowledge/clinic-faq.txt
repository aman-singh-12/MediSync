MediSync Clinic FAQ
Q: What are the clinic's operating hours?
A: The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Q: Do you accept walk-in patients?
A: We highly recommend booking an appointment to avoid long wait times, but we do accept walk-in patients for urgent non-life-threatening conditions.

Q: How can I access my medical records?
A: You can access your medical records anytime through the MediSync patient portal.
